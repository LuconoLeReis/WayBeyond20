New in v1.65.3

  1. A one-handed weapon now rolls its one-handed die. Clicking the smaller damage die on a Versatile weapon used to roll the two-handed die instead.
  2. Breath Weapon no longer offers a smite. Every smite is cast after hitting with a melee weapon or an unarmed strike, and a breath weapon is a saving throw with no attack roll.
  3. A weapon attack rolled from an item counts your Action, the same as one rolled from the Actions list.
  4. Extra Attack is understood. One Action buys the whole Attack action, so a second attack in the same turn no longer asks for a second Action.
  5. Finishing a long rest clears what has run out. Effects shorter than the rest end, concentration ends, and effects that outlast the rest are kept. Arming the rest button no longer resets anything before you confirm.
  6. Lay on Hands has a proper window. One touch and one bonus action: pick Healing, Purify Poison, or both, watch the pool total update as you allocate, and confirm on yourself or another creature. The 5 points for Purify Poison restore no hit points. It can be switched off per character.
  7. Declining a smite reads as declining. The buttons now say Add Smite and No Smite; saying no never calls off the attack.
  8. Damage from a second creature stays separate. Elemental Strike's second target no longer has its damage folded into the main target's total.

New in v1.65.2

  1. Named uses are spent on the right counter. A feature's use is matched to its own row on the sheet, so an option no longer spends a neighbouring pool, and an ordinary attack with no use of its own changes nothing.
  2. Paladin's Smite is marked used on D&D Beyond after a Divine Smite paid for with the free cast.
  3. Spell-slot smites spend the chosen slot even when the Elemental Strike follow-up changed sheet tabs first.
  4. A roll that cannot be sent spends nothing. After the extension is reloaded or updated, a roll from a stale D&D Beyond tab reports the problem and asks you to reload that tab instead of quietly consuming uses, slots or Channel Divinity.
  5. Simultaneous updates no longer overwrite each other. Attacking while hidden both clears Hidden and spends the Action; a concentration spell cast in combat spends the Action and keeps its Concentration tracking.
  6. The Smite check returns you to your tab, including when you have no smite, no fuel, or you cancel.
  7. Elemental Strike offers its four options as single-click buttons with the cost shown, works from the Actions list on its own, and leaves the native feature text intact.
  8. Draining Attack qualifies on the native Unarmed Strike and the Agile Strikes follow-up, and raises Temporary HP only when the new total is higher.
  9. Helper switches are per character.
  10. The Combat window no longer draws on the Character Builder.

Known limitations and test scope in v1.65.14

  1. The Lay on Hands pool does not stay reduced. D&D Beyond does not record changes to that counter, so the number returns when you reload the page. Track the pool yourself for now.
  2. The Concentration check helper opens when HP damage is entered while concentrating, or manually from the CONCENTRATION badge. Leave already-adjusted damage Unmodified. Conditional resistances require manual adjustment; unavailable roll results leave concentration unchanged.
  3. Damage components: Add source combines components for one concentration check; use separate checks for separate hits. The dialog never deducts HP again.
  4. Native casting scope: the shared casting bridge covers Cast and Use in the spell list, not separate sidebar slot buttons or item-charge controls. Use the spell-list buttons for integrated casting and resource tracking.
  5. Rolling only a damage die on a saving-throw action does not spend its use or Action.
  6. Draining Attack now waits for hit confirmation in managed attacks. Its revised damage ordering and critical/versatile combinations have automated coverage, but not all combinations have been retested live.
  7. Deferred damage requires the original character sheet to remain open without refreshing it before using Roll Damages.
  8. Live validation covered Chrome, D&D Beyond and Roll20. Foundry connection testing was outside this release's live scope.
  9. Additional coverage: all 17 automated suites passed. Native attack, save/damage, healing, buff and daily-use spell-list routes were tested live. Upcasts, ritual/display-only handling and temporary-HP spell branches have automated coverage, not equivalent live acceptance on every character or casting surface.

D&D Beyond character-sheet integration

  1. Quiet passive synchronization. Synchronizes sheet state without a missing-VTT error while preserving that warning for attempted rolls.
  2. New-feature discovery notices. Detects newly parsed class features, species traits, and feats without repeating notices on every scan.
  3. Safer roll interception. Limits roll handling to supported D&D Beyond roll targets.
  4. Reinjection and observer safeguards. Prevents duplicate WayBeyond20 listeners, observers, and pollers after page changes or reloads, including self-triggering Bardic Inspiration updates.
  5. Correct Game Log labels. Labels checks, saves, Initiative, attacks, healing, and other rolls appropriately.
  6. Local and Digital Dice result handling. Handles local custom rolls, Digital Dice totals, and feature callbacks.
  7. Critical-damage Game Log aggregation. Combines normal and critical phases into one correctly typed result.

Generic rules foundation

  1. Live Talent parser. Derives activation, components, duration, concentration, targeting, and effect data from current D&D Beyond text.
  2. Detailed targeting vocabulary. Records relationship, sensory, object, and creature-type targeting requirements.
  3. Known-target discovery and selection. Uses connected Roll20 turn-order creatures in supported target prompts.
  4. Remote effect delivery. Delivers supported effect records to the matching open D&D Beyond target sheet.
  5. Ordered weapon action pool. Separates base weapon damage and added effects with source, category, tags, and execution order.
  6. Target-specific roll formulas. Uses destination-appropriate dice syntax for supported feature formulas.

Effects, buffs, concentration, and character statistics

  1. Persistent active-effect records. Stores supported effects per character with their source, duration, concentration, flags, and mechanics.
  2. Concentration replacement. Replaces the prior tracked concentration effect when a new one begins.
  3. Buff and concentration badges. Displays contextual Buffs and Concentration information on the character sheet.
  4. Effect details and manual ending. Shows effect details and permits manual ending.
  5. Quiet local effect management. Avoids unnecessary effect-management messages in VTT chat.
  6. Armor Class adjustments. Displays reversible effect-based AC additions with their true source.
  7. Speed adjustments. Applies and displays supported effect-based speed multipliers.
  8. Haste mechanics. Supports Haste AC, speed, Dexterity-save Advantage, and Haste Action mechanics.
  9. Hide and Stealth effect. Creates a visible effect that grants Advantage to the next qualifying attack and is consumed only after the attack is dispatched.

Combat and action economy

  1. Local Combat Window. Shows Movement, Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, and optional Haste Action.
  2. Combat controls. Adds New Turn, End Combat, and a manual Combat Window menu command.
  3. Automatic combat start from Initiative. Starts local combat state from a qualifying Initiative roll.
  4. Activation-cost spending. Spends the correct combat resource after the associated roll is dispatched, using parsed activation metadata.
  5. Unavailable-resource warning. Warns about a spent resource and permits an explicit nonnegative override.
  6. Movement tracking. Displays and spends current movement, including supported speed changes.
  7. Roll20 token binding. Associates the D&D Beyond character with a selected or name-matched Roll20 token.
  8. Roll20 turn-order synchronization. Detects character turns, tracks rounds, resets combat resources, and prevents one open character sheet from spending another character's off-turn Action or Bonus Action.
  9. Roll20 target discovery. Exposes turn-order creatures to supported D&D Beyond target selection.
  10. Roll20 resolved-damage callback. Returns resolved damage to the matching D&D Beyond sheet for dependent features.

Hit Dice and rest handling

  1. Persistent Hit Dice tracker. Derives and stores remaining Hit Dice pools from current class levels.
  2. Hit Dice controls. Adds minus, plus, and Reset controls for d6, d8, d10, and d12 pools.
  3. Automatic Hit Die spending. Decrements the matching pool when a Hit Die is rolled.
  4. Long Rest reset handling. Resets WayBeyond20 Hit Dice after a completed Long Rest without duplicate listeners.

Feature-specific rules

  1. Triage Expert: Bedside Manner. Rolls one extra healing die and discards the lowest while retaining the modifier.
  2. Triage Expert: Blood and Bone. Adds Hit Die controls, target-die fallback, Bedside Manner integration, and Action spending.
  3. Draining Attack qualification. Marks qualifying natural-weapon attacks without applying Draining Attack to generic Unarmed Strikes, Bardic damage, or Agile Strikes.
  4. Draining Attack Temporary HP. Applies resolved qualifying damage as higher-only Temporary Hit Points through local, Digital Dice, and Roll20 paths.
  5. Bardic Inspiration native-use spending. Spends the correct native Bardic Inspiration use without matching unrelated controls.
  6. Bardic Inspiration feature card. Shows Bonus Action, range, die, remaining uses, and an Inspire button.
  7. Dazzling Footwork. Applies or recognizes Advantage on qualifying dancing Performance checks without redundant prompts.
  8. Agile Strikes continuation. Offers the optional attack and automatically chooses the strongest legal ability and damage profile.
  9. Savage Attacker. On Roll20, rolls the complete eligible base weapon-damage formula twice and keeps the higher total.
  10. Use Magic Device scroll casting. Offers and remembers an Intelligence-plus-proficiency Scrolls casting path for supported formulas.

Settings and diagnostics

  1. Display All Current Settings. Shows valid global, hidden, character, effect, Hit Dice, and combat settings.
  2. Persistent bounded Debug Log. Optionally records bounded diagnostics across parsing, rolls, effects, routing, Game Log, and callbacks.
  3. Debug Log controls. Provides Refresh, Copy, Clear, and Close controls.

Paladin feature support

  1. Native limited-use dispatch. Checks exact native counters before sending and marks a use only after dispatch, including shared Channel Divinity pools.
  2. Lay on Hands. Selects a healing amount, sends healing or cleansing information, and reduces the native D&D Beyond healing pool after dispatch.
  3. Oath of the Watchers: Watcher's Will. Sends only its own description, spends Action and Channel Divinity, and tracks one minute of Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.
  4. Oath of the Watchers: Abjure the Extraplanar. Sends only its own description with the live Wisdom save DC and spends Action and Channel Divinity.
  5. Oath of the Noble Genies: Elemental Smite. Preserves the distinct Dao, Djinni, Efreeti, and Marid after-Smite effects while using the shared Channel Divinity pool.
  6. Oath of the Noble Genies: Elemental Rebuke. Supports all five damage types, adds Charisma to 2d10, includes the Dexterity save, spends Reaction, and marks the native use.
  7. Oath of the Noble Genies: Noble Scion. Sends the Bonus Action, marks the native use, and tracks the ten-minute flight and Minor Wish reminder.
  8. Utility and defensive spell casting. Adds Cast on VTT beside Display so non-damaging spells can spend their activation and create persistent effects.
